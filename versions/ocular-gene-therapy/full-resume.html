Professional summary

Vitreoretinal surgeon and clinical strategist with 25+ years across medical and surgical retina, inherited retinal disorders, ROP, and tertiary retinal care. Author on genotype–phenotype correlation in IRDs and on next-generation sequencing / genetic counseling in ocular disorders. Built and led a multi-center tele-retina screening network (50+ centers, 10,000+ patient records) that mirrors the patient-identification, referral, and treatment-center infrastructure needed for ocular gene therapy. Active physician-educator (16+ supervised DNB theses, 120+ conference talks, DNB / NBE examiner) with a deep imaging interpretation footprint (OCT, OCTA, FAF, FA/ICG, B-scan).

Core fit: Inherited Retinal Disorders • Ocular Gene Therapy readiness • Patient Identification Pathways • Retinal Imaging Interpretation • Disease Progression & Monitoring • Referral Workflows • Treatment-Center Engagement • KOL & Physician Education
