Teaching Philosophy: Dr. Sherine has been an active educator for 25+ years, mentoring postgraduate students (DNB), teaching residents, and leading instruction courses at major ophthalmology conferences. Her teaching expertise spans surgical retina, ROP, advanced imaging, tele-ophthalmology, and emerging topics like AI in medical education. She serves as an examiner for DNB and NBE (National Board of Examination) assessments and provides mentorship to fellows in vitreoretinal surgery.
